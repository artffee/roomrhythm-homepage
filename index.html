How it works

One simple request.
One shared rhythm.

01 /

Guest taps.

A small medallion in each room, with a QR code as backup, opens that room's request page. There's nothing to download and nothing to sign up for.

02 /

Staff get it.

Requests for towels, cleaning, Do Not Disturb, or maintenance land in one shared queue, each tagged with its room number.

03 /

Everyone knows the status.

Staff accept the request, assign it, and mark it done, and the guest sees each update.

Privacy by design.

Independent hotels with roughly 10–60 rooms whose requests now arrive by phone calls, paper notes, or messaging apps.
